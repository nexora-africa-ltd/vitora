# Recovery Objectives Statement

For Vitora HMIS production services, Nexora Consulting Ltd adopts the following recovery objectives:

| Objective | Approved target | Definition |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Recovery Time Objective (RTO) | **4 hours** | Maximum target elapsed time from declaration of a recoverable service/data incident to restored, validated service availability. |
| Recovery Point Objective (RPO) | **24 hours** | Maximum accepted data-loss window, supported by the daily backup schedule. |

These are recovery targets, not automatic guarantees. Every recovery drill and incident must measure achieved recovery time and recovery point against these targets, record variances, and assign corrective actions.
