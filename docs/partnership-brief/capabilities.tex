\chapter*{How TibaBot Creates Value}

Artificial Intelligence creates value only when it improves clinical, operational and financial outcomes.

TibaBot has been designed as a modular Healthcare Intelligence Platform that delivers measurable value across the entire healthcare ecosystem. Each capability addresses a specific operational challenge while allowing HMIS providers to rapidly expand their product offering.

\vspace{8mm}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\subsection{Clinical Intelligence}

\begin{capability}

\textbf{Objective}

Support clinicians in making faster, more informed and evidence-based decisions while preserving clinician oversight.

\vspace{3mm}

\textbf{Capabilities}

\begin{itemize}

\item Clinical decision support

\item Differential diagnosis generation

\item Diagnostic reasoning assistance

\item Evidence-based recommendations

\item Risk stratification

\item Clinical summarisation

\item Longitudinal patient timeline generation

\item Early warning identification

\end{itemize}

\vspace{3mm}

\textbf{Business Value}

\begin{itemize}

\item Supports clinical consistency

\item Reduces information overload

\item Improves clinician productivity

\item Strengthens quality of care

\end{itemize}

\end{capability}
