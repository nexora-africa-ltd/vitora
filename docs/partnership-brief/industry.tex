\chapter*{Healthcare AI Has Become a Competitive Necessity}

Healthcare providers are no longer evaluating Artificial Intelligence as a future capability—they are increasingly expecting it as part of modern clinical software.

Across hospitals, clinicians and administrators are looking for systems that reduce documentation burden, improve clinical decision making, optimise operational efficiency and support better patient outcomes.

For Hospital Management Information System (HMIS) providers, this creates both an opportunity and a strategic challenge.

\begin{callout}

\textbf{The Question is No Longer}

\vspace{2mm}

\Large

"Should our HMIS include AI?"

\vspace{4mm}

\textbf{The Question is}

\vspace{2mm}

\Large

"How quickly can we deliver trustworthy AI capabilities to our customers?"

\end{callout}

\section{The Strategic Opportunity}

The opportunity extends beyond introducing a single AI feature.

Healthcare providers increasingly seek intelligent platforms capable of supporting clinicians throughout diagnosis, treatment, documentation, coding and operational decision making.

An HMIS enhanced with a robust healthcare Intelligence Platform can become significantly more differentiated within an increasingly competitive healthcare technology market.

Partnering with an established healthcare Intelligence Platform enables HMIS vendors to:
