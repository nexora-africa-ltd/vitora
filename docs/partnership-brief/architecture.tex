\section{What Is TibaBot?}

TibaBot is an enterprise Healthcare Intelligence Platform (HIP) designed to embed advanced artificial intelligence capabilities into existing healthcare systems.

Rather than functioning as another standalone application, TibaBot acts as an intelligence layer that augments existing Hospital Management Information Systems with continuously evolving AI services.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\section{Platform Positioning}

TibaBot has been designed to complement existing HMIS platforms rather than replace them.

The HMIS remains the authoritative System of Record, while TibaBot provides an extensible System of Intelligence that delivers clinical, operational and administrative AI capabilities through a secure integration layer.

\section{Five Intelligence Engines}

TibaBot is composed of five independent Intelligence Engines, each responsible for a distinct domain of healthcare intelligence.

Engines may be deployed individually or collectively depending on organisational requirements.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{center}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|Y|Y|}

\hline

\rowcolor{Primary!10}

\textbf{Intelligence Engine} &

\textbf{Purpose} \\

\hline

Clinical Intelligence Engine &

Clinical reasoning, decision support, differential diagnosis, risk prediction and patient summarisation. \\

\hline

Knowledge Intelligence Engine &

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using clinical guidelines, drug formularies, hospital SOPs, medical literature and institutional knowledge. \\

\hline

Documentation Intelligence Engine &

SOAP notes, encounter summaries, discharge summaries, referral letters and clinical documentation automation. \\

\hline

Diagnostic Intelligence Engine &

Laboratory interpretation, diagnostic reasoning, trend analysis and clinical correlation. \\

\hline

Workflow Intelligence Engine &

Administrative automation, coding assistance, revenue cycle optimisation, reporting and workflow orchestration. \\

\hline

\end{tabularx}

\end{center}

\section{Request Lifecycle}

A typical request flows through the platform as follows:

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{center}

\resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%

\node[flowstep](user){Clinician Action};

\node[flowstep,right=5mm of hmisStep](fabricStep){Integration Fabric};

\node[flowstep,fill=Accent!12,draw=Accent,right=5mm of fabricStep](engine){Intelligence Engine};

\node[flowstep,fill=Success!8,draw=Success,right=5mm of engine](gov){AI Governance};

\draw[-Latex,thick](user)--(hmisStep);

\draw[-Latex,thick](hmisStep)--(fabricStep);

\draw[-Latex,thick](fabricStep)--(engine);

\draw[-Latex,thick](engine)--(gov);

\draw[-Latex,thick](gov.south)--(response.north);

\draw[-Latex,thick](response.west)-|(fabricStep.south);

\draw[-Latex,thick](fabricStep.west)-|(hmisStep.south);

\end{tikzpicture}%
}

\end{center}

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{enumerate}

\item A clinician action within the HMIS triggers an event (e.g. patient lookup, order entry, documentation).

\item The Integration Fabric routes the event to the appropriate Intelligence Engine.

\item The Engine processes the request using its specialised models and knowledge base.

\item The AI Governance Layer enforces security, audit and compliance policies before the response is returned.

\item The response flows back through the Integration Fabric to the HMIS, where it is presented to the clinician.

\end{enumerate}

All interactions are logged, monitored and available for audit. The entire lifecycle completes within the HMIS workflow — clinicians never leave their familiar interface.

\section{Deployment Models}

TibaBot supports multiple deployment approaches to accommodate different partner infrastructure preferences and regulatory requirements.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{center}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|Y|Y|Y|}

\hline

\rowcolor{Primary!10}

\textbf{Model} &

\textbf{Description} &

\textbf{Use Case} \\

\hline

Nexora Cloud &

Fully managed by Nexora. Partner connects via secure APIs. &

Fastest time to value. Minimal infrastructure overhead. \\

\hline

Dedicated Instance &

Isolated single-tenant deployment managed by Nexora. &

Enhanced data isolation for enterprise requirements. \\

\hline

Partner Cloud &

Deployed within the partner's cloud environment. &

Maximum data residency control. Regulatory compliance. \\

\hline

Hybrid &

Core platform in Nexora cloud, sensitive workloads on-premise or in partner cloud. &

Flexible balance of control and managed operations. \\

\hline

\end{tabularx}

\end{center}

Each deployment model includes the full TibaBot platform — all Intelligence Engines, Platform Services, the Integration Fabric and the AI Governance Layer. The choice of model affects data residency, latency and operational responsibility, not functional capability.
