\chapter*{Healthcare Intelligence Platform Architecture}

TibaBot has been designed as a Healthcare Intelligence Platform (HIP) that complements existing Hospital Management Information Systems rather than replacing them.

The HMIS remains the authoritative System of Record, while TibaBot provides an extensible System of Intelligence that delivers clinical, operational and administrative AI capabilities through a secure integration layer.

\section{The Five Platform Layers}

\begin{center}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|Y|Y|}

\hline

\rowcolor{Primary!10}

\textbf{Layer}

&

\textbf{Purpose}

\\

\hline

Clinical Intelligence Engine

&

Clinical reasoning, decision support, documentation, diagnostics and patient summarisation.

\\

\hline

Knowledge Intelligence Engine

&

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using clinical guidelines, drug formularies, SOPs, medical literature and institutional knowledge.

\\

\hline

Workflow Intelligence Engine

&

Administrative automation, coding assistance, revenue cycle optimisation, reporting and workflow orchestration.

\\

\hline

Integration Fabric

&

Secure interoperability with HMIS platforms through REST APIs, HL7, FHIR, SDKs and event-driven integrations.

\\

\hline

AI Governance Layer

&

Security, auditability, explainability, monitoring, evaluation, access control and responsible AI governance.

\\

\hline

\end{tabularx}

\section{Designed for Enterprise Healthcare}

Unlike point solutions that solve a single problem, TibaBot has been architected as a reusable intelligence platform.

New AI capabilities can be introduced by extending one or more intelligence engines without requiring hospitals to replace or redesign their existing HMIS.

This enables continuous innovation while protecting previous technology investments.
