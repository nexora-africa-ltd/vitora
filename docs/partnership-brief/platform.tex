\chapter*{The TibaBot Platform}

TibaBot is an enterprise Healthcare Intelligence Platform designed to embed advanced artificial intelligence capabilities into existing healthcare systems.

Rather than functioning as another standalone application, TibaBot acts as an intelligence layer that augments existing HMIS platforms with continuously evolving AI services.

\vspace{6mm}

\begin{callout}

\centering

\Large

\textbf{Existing HMIS + TibaBot = AI-Native Healthcare Platform}

\end{callout}

\section{Modular by Design}

Unlike monolithic AI assistants, TibaBot is composed of independent modules that can be deployed individually or collectively depending on organisational requirements.

\vspace{4mm}

\begin{center}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|Y|Y|}

\hline

\section{Designed for Continuous Innovation}

One of the greatest advantages of TibaBot is that new capabilities can be introduced without requiring hospitals to replace their existing HMIS.

As the platform evolves, partners gain access to new AI services through platform updates rather than major software redevelopment projects.
