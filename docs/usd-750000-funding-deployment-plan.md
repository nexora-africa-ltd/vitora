# US$750,000 Funding Deployment Plan

## Nexora Consulting Limited | Vitora & TibaBot AI

# Purpose and investment logic

## Preamble

Nexora Consulting Limited is seeking **US$750,000** to establish repeatable commercialization of Vitora, introduce patient-facing telemedicine within Vitora, and develop a focused set of locally relevant AI and care-coordination capabilities. Our proposed raise increases the original US$250,000 requirement by **US$500,000**, with an indicative execution horizon of **18 months**.

Vitora is Nexora Consulting Limited’s healthcare information platform. Its facility-facing capabilities support clinical records, diagnostics, pharmacy, billing, reporting, and administrative workflows. Its patient-facing development will include registration, appointments, telemedicine consultations, access to relevant records, referrals, and follow-up. **Telemedicine is a patient-facing feature of Vitora.**

TibaBot is Nexora’s AI layer, supporting selected patient and clinician workflows within Vitora. Its funded development focuses on structured intake assistance, clinical transcription, reviewable documentation, evidence-grounded assistance, and selected image-based capabilities. Clinical consultations, appointments, payments, referral coordination, and continuing-care services belong to Vitora’s product and service workflows.

Our investment strategy is to strengthen the commercial foundation while developing a limited number of features that can demonstrate practical value. Vitora remains our principal route to facility revenue and customer distribution. Patient-facing services will extend the usefulness of those deployments, while TibaBot will support the quality and efficiency of selected tasks.

Kenya is our primary execution market. We will fund Kenya-led transcription development and limited partner-led research and language benchmarking in Rwanda, Ghana, and South Africa. We will evaluate broader regional operations, comprehensive multilingual coverage, and additional production medical-imaging products as subsequent investment opportunities.

## Planning basis

Our allocations are **indicative planning envelopes**. We will refine and substantiate them through detailed staffing costs, product-readiness assessment, customer economics, data-access agreements, and supplier quotations before committing expenditure.

Our budget assumes substantial reuse of the core platform, staged hiring, and adoption of existing models and services where they meet our requirements. We will commission custom training or fine-tuning where evaluation identifies a performance, licensing, deployment, or economic gap.

The original US$250,000 is included in our revised total as the foundation allocation; it does not represent a statement of funding already received or spent. We will reconcile existing cash, prior expenditure, and outstanding commitments when finalising our net funding requirement and deployment schedule.

## Revenue and cost assumptions

Our initial facility prices are KES 9,000 per month for clinics, KES 50,000 for hospitals, and KES 80,000 for enterprise customers. Add-on prices and attach rates are modelled separately and generate approximately **US$39.42 of expected monthly add-on revenue per active facility** in the initial base case. We will validate these assumptions through customer cohorts.

The model applies a one-month free pilot, 55% base conversion, and an illustrative 1% monthly churn rate to opening customers. Subscription revenue uses average active customers during each month; exit ARR uses closing facility subscription and add-on MRR. Expected customer counts may be fractional. Implementation revenue defaults to zero while onboarding and acquisition remain explicit costs. We will validate exceptional migration charges separately.

Vitora telemedicine initially uses a provisional KES 1,200 patient fee and KES 900 clinician payment per consultation. The workbook supports net-agent or gross-principal presentation, subject to contract review, while preserving the same cash receipts, provider payments, and EBITDA. Continuous-care pricing and clinical costs are separate pilot assumptions. Additional revenue from future regional launches, broad imaging products, or separately priced referral modules is not included.

We allocate loaded staff costs and non-payroll expenditure to individual programmes once. Equipment purchases are capital expenditure with depreciation; rented training compute and development work are expensed. Our tax and collection schedules are simplified planning assumptions that we will refine for the applicable contracts and obligations.
