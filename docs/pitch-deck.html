Security & Compliance

Patient data protection is
the foundation

Six pillars of protection — designed for Kenya's regulatory landscape from day one.

1

AES Encryption at Rest & TLS in Transit

Fernet/AES-128 for PII fields (national ID, phone), TLS 1.3 everywhere

2

Role-Based Access Control

12+ roles, sensitive records (HIV/GBV) need explicit permission

3

Full Audit Trail

Every access logged — user, IP, timestamp, purpose. 7-year retention

MFA Enforcement
